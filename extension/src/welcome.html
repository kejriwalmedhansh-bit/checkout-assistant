Up to 20% off, at shops you already use

Plenty of Indian shops sell their own gift vouchers below face value — around 9% on a typical shop, more on the best. Buy the voucher, spend it on the order you were placing anyway, pay less. Same shop, same order, same delivery.

To find yours, Dealo has to be able to look at the shop pages you open.

  • Shop pages you are checking out on
  • The order total shown on those pages
  • Never your email, messages or files
  • Never your card details

Chrome will ask you to confirm. You can turn this off any time.
